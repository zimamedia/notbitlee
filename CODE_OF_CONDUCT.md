# Code of conduct

NotBitLee is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through [Zima Media](https://zimamedia.com). Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block people who break this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
