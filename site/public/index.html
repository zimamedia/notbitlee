How it works

One click to deploy. No hosting to manage.

  1. 1

    Click “Deploy to Cloudflare”

    Cloudflare copies the code to your GitHub, creates your database and asks for the email and password you will sign in with.

  2. 2

    Sign in and make it yours

    Add your logo and colour, invite your team, and import the links you already have.

  3. 3

    Point your domain at it

    Attach a short domain like go.yourbrand.com in the Cloudflare dashboard. Until then, links work on your free workers.dev address.

Questions

Is it really free?

The software is free and open source. It runs on Cloudflare Workers, and Cloudflare's free plan covers most small teams and agencies. Each redirect counts as one request against Cloudflare's daily limits. If you get serious traffic, Cloudflare's paid Workers plan raises those limits. You pay Cloudflare directly; we never charge you.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The deploy button does the setup for you. You type an email and a password, then click Deploy. Developers can still change anything they like, because the code is theirs.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Add any domain in your Cloudflare account to the app as a custom domain and every link uses it. You can keep the dashboard on the same domain or give it its own, such as links.yourbrand.com.

Can I move my existing links over?

Yes. Export your links as a CSV from your current tool and upload it in Settings. Links on your own custom domain keep working once that domain points to NotBitLee.

Who can see my links and clicks?

Only you and the people you add. The data sits in a database on your Cloudflare account. We have no access to it and no copy of it.

Can I resell it to my clients?

NotBitLee is MIT licensed, so you can deploy it for clients, brand it as your own and charge for your service. Keep the licence file in the code.
